# Ohara Support

Ohara is a tournament log for the One Piece Card Game. It is made by one person.

## Getting help

Email **buckelew.02@gmail.com**. Say what phone you are on and what happened, and
you will get a reply.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Tap "Continue without an account" on the first screen and everything works —
logging, stats, import, export. An account only adds syncing your log to your
other devices.

**Where is my log kept?**
On your phone. It is not sent anywhere unless you sign in and press Sync, and
then it goes only to your own account.

**Will I lose my log if I delete the app?**
Yes, unless you exported a backup or signed in and synced. Settings has an
Export that writes a file you can keep.

**What happens to a screenshot I import?**
It is read on your phone and the text is kept. The picture is not uploaded and
Ohara does not keep a copy of it.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings, then Delete account. It removes the account and everything synced to
the server. The log on your phone is left alone.
